Product Details

Sentron 5SY Series Miniature circuit breakers

Miniature circuit breakers from Siemens, offer the highest quality and reliability. The excellent technical properties of the devices mean you are always on the safe side. Apart from that you benefit from the innovative mounting options which enable quick and easy installation. The miniature circuit breakers from Siemens can be combined as required with a wide range of supplementary components e.g. control current switches, fault signal contacts, shunt releases, under voltage releases, remote operating mechanisms or with RCCB units to provide protection for persons and against fire.

Features and Benefits

Enclosure: IP20 equipment
Automatic cut-out, 1, 2, 3 and 4-pole
DIN rail assembly
For use in industry, and in the functional and residential construction sector
Supports fast and convenient removal from the assembly

Standards

IEC 60898 - Relates to Low Voltage Circuit Breakers for use in household and similar
Installations. In the UK traditionally known as miniature circuit breakers
(MCB’s).

Q: What is the differences in the Tripping Curves B, C, and D?

A: Curve B: Tripping: 3 to 5 times the rated current.
Applications: protection of generators, persons, very long cables
Curve C: Tripping: 5 to 10 times the rated current
Applications: protection of circuits and general applications
Curve D: Tripping: 10 to 14 times the rated current
Applications: protection of high surge circuits, welders, transformers and motors

Standards

EN 60898, VDE 0641 part of 11, IEC 60898
